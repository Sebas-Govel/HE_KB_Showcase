Future HCI

Beyond the Keyboard: The Future of Human Interaction

This keyboard is only our opening chapter. Our broader vision reimagines how humans interact with machines through three foundational vectors:

Vector 01

Spatial Bezel-Tracking Eye Glasses

Lightweight open-hardware glasses combining pupil gaze tracking with miniature outward sensors that detect computer monitor bezels in 3D space, mapping natural gaze directly to cursor coordinates across multi-screen setups without head movement.

Vector 02

Capacitive Surface (Touchpad) Lightweight Mouse

An ultra-lightweight ergonomic mouse shell integrating a continuous capacitive touchpad surface, enabling fluid multi-finger gesture navigation, horizontal panning, and pressure-sensitive analog input without mechanical switches.

Vector 03

Non-Invasive Continuous Forms of Input

Exploring subtle wearable interface devices—such as gyroscopic spatial rings, gestural smart wristbands, and micro-movement sensors—for effortless, ambient, and hands-free computing control.
